a stream of consciousness

The internet is an organism. It is ferociously alive with networks of learning and love and loss.
The web is an entangled mess of all of us- one big telephone book. Different people, different cultures, differences everywhere that start to look like similarities against the backdrop of a flickering screen.

To be on the internet has a complexity to it. The web provides new lives with accessibility, provides new challenges too.
It has the evils and the hearts of all we know so far. It resembles our planet, our mark.

The web is filled with the rise and fall of your breath as you rest your laptop on your chest.

Digital experiences have the rush of a wave like emotions for anything else.
People can tear their heart out on a YouTube comment or feel immense catharsis pressing send on a message.

The web is full of desire, opulence and curiosity. There is a space for everyone and everything. Niche communities can become a second home.

The web deserves nourishment. It is, at times, starved of creativity in favour for profit.
The internet spearheads communication and in that lies business. Business lies in the same bed with entertainment, learning, love and all the rest (it is getting cramped).

The internet is a living thing, For me it transcribes a start and end. A fully realised journey of my life and my work in one way or another exists here- floating.
My favourite colour, can be found in images. My change in name documented by change in usernames. My schools’ websites, my dogs rescue shelter, clips from the cartoon my cat is named after. Relics of a piece of a much bigger museum-

something that has its own life in holding space for all of ours.
